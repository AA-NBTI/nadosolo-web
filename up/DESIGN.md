## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, sophisticated editorial atmosphere tailored for meaningful real-world connection. It balances the cultural polish of an independent food and lifestyle journal with the warmth and approachability needed to disarm solo dining attendees.

### Personality & Values
- **Intimate & Welcoming:** Avoids clinical dating app tropes in favor of supper club hospitality, candlelit warmth, and curated ease.
- **Editorial Poise:** Utilizes literary typographic pacing, ample negative space, and disciplined layout rhythm reminiscent of print magazines.
- **Grounded Tactility:** Surfaces evoke natural parchment, linen, warm stone, and terracotta tableware.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Editorial Minimalism** and **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous cream-washed canvases replace sterile whites.
- Deep espresso ink tones substitute harsh absolute blacks.
- Vibrant terracotta and wine-rose accents signal culinary energy and Friday evening anticipation without aggressive urgency.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in natural dining room tones—parchment linen, aged timber, steeped wine, and sunbaked clay.

### Role Mapping
- **Canvas & Backdrops:** `#FAF8F5` provides a luminous, warm cream foundation that reduces glare and establishes an analog print quality.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Layer `#FFFFFF` for primary elevated surfaces, subtly bordered with stone neutrals (`#E7E5E4`).
- **Typography & High-Contrast Elements:** `#1C1917` (Deep Espresso) serves as primary ink for titles, body copy, and heavy strokes. Secondary text relies on `#57534E` (Warm Slate/Pebble).
- **Primary Accent (`#BE123C` Deep Rose-Wine):** Reserved for intentional calls-to-action, active booking statuses, and signature emblems.
- **Secondary Accent (`#EA580C` Warm Terracotta):** Highlights conversational prompts, table spots remaining, and festive micro-indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#78350F` Spiced Amber):** Applied to culinary curation tags, neighborhood badges, and host remarks.

## Typography

The type system blends literary classicism with ergonomic digital clarity.

### Pairing Rationale
- **Playfair Display:** Sets a romantic, curated tone for marquee statements, table themes, and narrative hooks. It evokes food and wine journals, lending stature to the Friday evening ritual.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides friendly geometry, immaculate legibility at small sizes, and open counters. It keeps venue logistics, seat selections, and dietary questionnaires effortless to navigate.

### Typographic Rules
- Set uppercase styling only on `label-sm` metadata tags, badges, and status labels, pairing them with generous tracking (+0.08em).
- Reserve italic variants of `Playfair Display` exclusively for accentuation (e.g., *"Table of Six"*, *"Curated Conversation"*). Never italicize entire paragraphs.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts prioritize breathing room, deliberate reading cadences, and clear content chunking.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1180px, flanked by `margin` (3rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Narrative sidebars and table itineraries occupy a 4:8 or 5:7 column split.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins, transitioning complex booking multi-columns into progressive, stacked panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout with `margin-mobile` (1.25rem) and `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Navigation docks to a persistent, tactile bottom sheet or floating bar for effortless one-handed thumb interaction.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Apply `space-xl` between modular editorial story blocks and date/neighborhood selectors.
- Maintain `space-md` internally within cards, keeping interactive tags, attendee counters, and descriptions unified.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated as warm, diffused light hitting tactile paper stock, avoiding cool tech-centric glows or heavy synthetic drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#FAF8F5` flat background tone.
- **Card Surface (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a hair-thin perimeter border (`1px solid #E7E5E4`) and an ambient, warm-tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Controls & Modals (Level 2):** Elevated interactive elements (such as RSVP sticky bars or table matching sheets) utilize a broader, candlelit dispersion:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Active / Dragged Overlays (Level 3):**
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(190, 18, 60, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects warm ceramic plates and tailored paper ephemera: softly curved, balanced, and never overly mechanical.

### Corner Radius System
- Standard structural elements adopt `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px) for buttons, text inputs, and small menus.
- Content containers, itinerary blocks, and imagery frames step up to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for an inviting, tactile finish.
- Hero spotlight cards and bottom sheets leverage `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px).
- Status tags, seat indicators, and avatar silhouettes utilize full circular or pill radii (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled `#BE123C` (Rose-Wine) with crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded` (8px), styled with `label-md`. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#9F1239` with a subtle 1px upward transform.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E7E5E4` border, `#1C1917` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F5F2EB` and border transitions to `#D6D3D1`.
- **Warm Terracotta Action:** Reserved for high-urgency reservations (e.g., *"1 Seat Left"*), using `#EA580C` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards
- **Dinner Event Card:** Pure white background, `1px solid #E7E5E4` border, `rounded-lg` (16px). Features an aspect-ratio-locked imagery frame, editorial serif neighborhood header, and metadata cluster (time, cuisine style, host tone).
- **Interactive State:** Hover lifts the card with a warm terracotta shadow tint and transitions the border to `#D6D3D1`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curated Trait Tag:** Background `#F5F2EB`, text `#57534E`, border `#E7E5E4`, pill-shaped, using `label-sm` tracking.
- **Status Indicator:** Pill container with `#FFF1F2` (soft blush) background, `#BE123C` text, paired with a miniature 6px terracotta pulse dot.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5E4`, text `#1C1917`, placeholder `#A8A29E`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#BE123C` with an ambient glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(190, 18, 60, 0.12)`. No harsh browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular selection rings, `20px` diameter, `1.5px solid #D6D3D1`. When checked, surfaces fill with `#BE123C` housing a centered 8px white circle.
- **Checkboxes:** `rounded` (4px), filling `#BE123C` on selection with an off-white check icon.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **The "Table of Six" Seat Visualizer:** A stylized miniature table diagram with six circular nodes showing filled seats (warm terracotta dots), your reserved seat (blush ring with icon), and remaining open spots (dashed outline circles).
- **Dietary & Vibe Selector:** Square-proportioned selectable tiles with subtle culinary iconography, transitioning from `#FAF8F5` surface to `#FFF1F2` with a `#BE123C` border when toggled.
- **Friday Reveal Countdown:** A quiet editorial timestamp block featuring tabular serif figures framed within a warm linen container.
